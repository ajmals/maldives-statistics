Definitions:
Local calls: Calls within the same zone, excluding mobile calls
Inter-zone calls: Calls between zones within the national boundary, excluding mobile calls
Fixed-->Mob calls: calls from fixed lines to mobiles
Mobile calls:  Calls mobile to mobile and mobile to fixed lines from both Operators
International calls: International calls from fixed lines and mobiles

Crisis Timeline & Structural Policy Innovations

Key institutional milestones that guided the nation through the pandemic.

Milestone Log

The Greater Malé Connectivity Revolution

Bridging the archipelago's economic core across deep ocean channels.

Infrastructure
Sep 2018
Sinamalé China-Maldives Friendship Bridge: 1.39 km multi-lane bridge connecting Malé with Hulhulé Airport and Hulhumalé. Eliminated reliance on 24/7 ferries and unlocked Hulhumalé housing.
2020–2022
Hulhumalé Phase 2 Colonization: Hiyaa public housing scheme (16 towers, 7,000 apartments) populated ~40,000 residents, decompressing Malé's overcrowded rental market.
2024–2026
Thilamalé Bridge (GMCP - 6.74 km): Connecting Malé to Villimalé, Gulhifalhu (new international port), and Thilafushi (industrial relocation), completing the unified metropolitan ring.
Ongoing
Rasmale Eco-Smart Reclamation: Fushidhiggaru Falhu reclamation project aiming to add 1,150 hectares of zero-carbon urban space for future generations.

Demographic Transition
